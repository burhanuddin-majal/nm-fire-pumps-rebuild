ABOUT NM FIRE

Built around one purpose:
protecting what matters.

CNP NM Fire-fighting System Co., Ltd. manufactures fire pumps, diesel engines, controllers, motor- and diesel-driven pump sets, and packaged fire-pump systems for domestic and international markets.

The company describes its mission as producing high-standard products with advanced technology to “Protect life, love and history”.
