NMFIRE — Protect life, love, and history

22 April 2026

Modern fire protection is essential to protecting people, communities and the places that carry our history. NMFire presents its work as more than manufacturing pumps: it aims to provide dependable equipment, engineering and peace of mind.

The company says it is the first and only manufacturer in China able to supply UL/FM fire pumps and diesel engines together. Its product range includes pumps, diesel engines, gearboxes and couplings designed to meet relevant NFPA 20, UL and FM requirements.

NMFire describes its testing and production facilities as including an 8,000 m³ test pool, a 50-tonne crane, and test power up to 5,000 kW. Its diesel engine range is stated as 40–1,207 HP. The company says its systems serve projects in more than 100 countries, including airports, industrial facilities, high-rise buildings, metro systems and data centres.

The story closes with the company’s guiding message: “Protect Life. Love. And History.”

NM FIRE and DESKAR Build a Secure Future Together in Istanbul

5 March 2025

NM FIRE and its strategic partner DESKAR held the “Protecting Lives, Inheriting Civilizations” Fire Safety Technology Summit at the Swissôtel by the Bosphorus in Istanbul.

More than 200 attendees joined, including design institute specialists, construction contractors, investment organisations and government fire department representatives from across Turkey. The event explored modern fire pump systems and their role in protecting cultural heritage and industrial facilities.

The article connects Istanbul’s historic landmarks with the safety needs of modern industrial developments. It describes cooperation between NM FIRE and Turkish partners as a way to support fire protection across both historic and contemporary environments.

NMFIRE Product Promotion and Technical Seminar in Bangladesh

24 October 2019

In October 2019, the NMFIRE Bangladesh team hosted a product promotion and technical seminar at the Radisson Blu Dhaka Water Garden Hotel. The event brought together 93 participants representing 65 organisations, including fire service representatives, design institutes, existing customers, fire engineering companies, textile-industry groups and end users.

Mustafizur Rahman, chairman of Avenue Trade & Engineering, welcomed attendees and discussed challenges in the local fire protection market, including understanding of UL/FM-certified pumps, product availability, standards and after-sales service.

NMFIRE’s Tong Chunguang introduced the company and its products and joined discussions about marketing, technology and service. The seminar aimed to share technical knowledge and promote more consistent fire protection standards, particularly in industries where fire risks are significant.

Reap As You Sow — NMFIRE 2019 International Training Conference

24 July 2019

Six years after its establishment, NMFIRE described its progress toward becoming a recognised company in the fire pump industry, including its UL/FM fire pump certification and UL certification for fire diesel engines.

The company established an international training school to help design engineers, dealers, businesses and end users understand the role of UL and FM standards in fire protection systems and the importance of selecting and installing suitable equipment.

The annual international training conference took place at the Changsha factory on 9 May 2019. The three-day programme welcomed representatives from 18 fire protection companies across 11 countries. Sessions covered the differences between listed and non-listed fire pumps, fire diesel engines and standard diesel engines, and the design and installation of fire pump packages. Guests also toured the factory and witness-testing laboratory.

The article emphasises training, safety awareness, knowledge-sharing and long-term commitment to protecting lives and property.

NM Fire Broke the Long-term Monopoly Situation of Fire Products

28 June 2019

The article discusses the development of fire protection technology and the importance of recognised standards. At the 17th International Fire Protection Equipment Technology Conference & Exposition in Beijing, CNP NM Fire Fighting System Co., Ltd received a UL certificate for fire-fighting engines, which the company described as a first for a Chinese enterprise.

It also recounts the company’s certification milestones: becoming the first enterprise in China to hold both UL and FM international certifications for fire pumps in March 2017, followed by UL certification for its diesel fire engine in September of that year.

The company says these achievements helped expand the availability of certified fire protection products from Chinese manufacturers and supported its efforts to serve international markets.
