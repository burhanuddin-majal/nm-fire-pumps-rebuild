POLICIES & LEGAL

Privacy Policy

Privacy matters. We know that data privacy is a top issue today, and we want you to enjoy your interaction with us whilst knowing that we value your Personal Data and protect it.

Here you will find an overview of how we process your Personal Data, the purposes for which we process it, and how you benefit. You’ll also see what your rights are and how you can contact us.

Updates to this Privacy Notice

As business and technology evolves, we might need to change this Privacy Notice. We encourage you to regularly review this Privacy Notice to stay up to date with how CNP NM Fire-fighting System Co., Ltd. uses your Personal Data.

Aged under 13?

If you’re under the age of 13, we kindly ask you to wait until you are a bit older to interact with us, or ask a parent or guardian to contact us. We can’t collect and use your Personal Data without their agreement.

Why do we process your Personal Data?

We process your Personal Data, including any sensitive personal data you have provided to us with your consent, to communicate with you, fulfil your purchase orders, answer your queries and provide communications about CNP NM Fire-fighting System Co., Ltd. and our products. We also process your Personal Data to help us comply with the law, sell or transfer any relevant part of our business, manage our systems and finances, conduct investigations and exercise legal rights. We combine your Personal Data from all sources so that we can better understand you and improve and personalize your experience when interacting with us.

Who can access your Personal Data and why?

We limit disclosure of your Personal Data, but may disclose it in certain instances, mainly to:

  • Companies within CNP NM Fire-fighting System Co., Ltd., where required for our legitimate interests or with your consent.
  • Third parties engaged to provide services, such as administering company websites, applications and services (including features, programs and promotions), subject to appropriate protections.
  • Credit reporting agencies or debt collectors, where permitted by law and where we need to verify creditworthiness (for example, for orders on invoice) or collect outstanding invoices.
  • Relevant public agencies and authorities, where required by law or a legitimate business interest.

Data security and retention

We use a variety of measures to keep your Personal Data confidential and secure, including restricting access on a need-to-know basis and following appropriate security standards to protect your data.

We take every reasonable step to ensure your Personal Data is processed only for the minimum period necessary in connection with: (i) the purposes set out in this Privacy Notice; (ii) any additional purposes notified to you at or before collection of the relevant Personal Data or commencement of the relevant processing; or (iii) as required or permitted by applicable law; and thereafter, for the duration of any applicable limitation period. Once your Personal Data is no longer required, we will destroy or delete it securely.

Contact us

CNP NM Fire-fighting System Co., Ltd. acts as the “controller” of your Personal Data processed under or in connection with this Privacy Notice. If you have questions or comments regarding this Privacy Notice or the company’s Personal Data collection and processing practices, please contact us or email pakistan@nmfirepump.com.
