# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The NM Fire Website Rebuild project welcomes constructive, respectful participation. We aim to keep project discussions, issue reports, reviews, and contributions useful, safe, and professional for everyone.

## Expected Behaviour

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and technical decisions without personal attacks.
- Give feedback that focuses on the work, not on a person's identity or background.
- Accept reasonable corrections and differing viewpoints professionally.
- Share accurate information and clearly label assumptions or unverified claims.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish personal information, credentials, private emails, access tokens, or confidential business material.
- Follow GitHub's terms and the repository's license and contribution requirements.
- Use project issues and discussions responsibly; avoid spam, harassment, threats, or deliberately disruptive behaviour.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, repeated unwanted contact, publishing private information without permission, malicious spam, and knowingly submitting deceptive or harmful project changes.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including repository issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, and project-related communications when someone is representing or participating in this project.

## Reporting a Concern

Report concerns privately to **complains@majal.dpdns.org**. Include relevant links and a concise description of the concern. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information unless it is necessary to explain the issue.

Project maintainers will review reports as fairly and confidentially as reasonably possible. They may ask for clarification and take proportionate action, including editing or removing content, closing discussions, restricting participation, or blocking an account. The project cannot guarantee absolute confidentiality or a particular outcome.

For website, business, or licensing enquiries, use the contact details provided in the relevant project documentation rather than using the conduct-reporting address.

## Enforcement Principles

Maintainers will consider the context, impact, and available evidence when responding to a report. They should communicate decisions where appropriate and apply these standards consistently.

## Acknowledgement

By participating in this project, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct and GitHub's applicable policies.
