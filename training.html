ABOUT US

NM Fire Training Center

NM Fire jointly built ‘Fire Pump Technology Training Center’ together with Domestic Fire Protection Association and Overseas Certificate Authority for further utilizing global advanced fire pump technology and changing backward situation of fire pump field.

The training center is currently being built. NM Fire specially completed a standard package system for the training center, which can be used for on-the-spot teaching and practical operation training.

Trainees

  • Designers from firefighting engineering company and designing institute
  • Government employees who engage in fire control management
  • Fire pump dealers
  • Fire pump group end users and operators

What can you get from training?

  • Operating principles of package system
  • Operating procedure of package system
  • Technical standard and technology training for fire pumps, firefighting diesel engines and controllers, etc.
