01 / WITNESS TESTING

Testing capability for critical fire-protection equipment

The official NM Fire Strength page describes the company as the first authorized by UL CCIC as a witness testing centre for both fire pumps and firefighting engines.

It also highlights two witness laboratories associated with UL 448 for fire pumps and UL 1247 for diesel engines. For project approvals, always confirm the current listing and scope directly with the relevant certification body.
