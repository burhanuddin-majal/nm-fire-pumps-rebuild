WHO WE ARE

Fire-protection systems, backed by engineering.

CNP NM Fire-fighting System Co., Ltd. is a manufacturer of fire pumps, diesel engines, controllers and complete fire-pump packages designed for domestic and international fire-protection requirements.

The company describes a technical team of engineers and international specialists with more than 20 years of research and development experience in fire-fighting equipment. Its range includes pump sets driven by electric motors or diesel engines, as well as packaged and containerised systems.

NMFIRE is also listed as a member of the European Fire Sprinkler Network (EFSN). For every project, confirm the exact model, configuration and applicable approvals against the current manufacturer documentation.
