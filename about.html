COMPANY PROFILE

CNP NM Fire-fighting System Co., Ltd.

CNP NM Fire-fighting System Co., Ltd. describes itself as a manufacturer of fire pumps, diesel engines, controllers, diesel-engine-driven and electric-motor-driven fire pump sets, and fire-pump package and containerised package systems.

The company states that its technical team includes engineers and international experts with experience in research and development for fire-fighting equipment. The official website also describes products carrying certifications from organisations including UL, FM, APSAD and SETSCO. Approval status must always be checked for the particular model and assembly.
