SAFETY FIRST

Build a plan around
people and property.

Fire protection needs differ by building use, occupancy, layout, water supply, local regulations and project requirements. Start with a review of the site and the risks that need to be addressed.

01
⌕

Risk assessment

Review the building, its use and potential fire hazards to help identify suitable protection priorities.

02
◈

System planning

Consider appropriate alarms, sprinklers, extinguishers, fire pumps and suppression systems for the project.

03
✓

Testing & maintenance

Plan for commissioning, inspection, maintenance and future upgrades so systems can be checked over time.

HOW IT WORKS

From review to
ongoing readiness.

A structured process helps teams make informed decisions and coordinate the work of building owners, designers, installers and qualified specialists.

STEP 01

Understand the site

Share the building type, floor plans where available, occupancy, location and project objectives.

STEP 02

Review requirements

Assess hazards and identify applicable local codes, authority requirements and project specifications with qualified professionals.

STEP 03

Plan and verify

Review proposed equipment and system design, then arrange appropriate installation checks, testing and maintenance.

Fire protection designs and compliance decisions must be confirmed by appropriately qualified professionals and the relevant local authorities. This page is general information, not a substitute for a site-specific engineering assessment.
